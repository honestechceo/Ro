RO Log Privacy Policy

Last updated November 11, 2026

The short version

RO Log keeps your repair orders, hours, pay settings, photos and files on your iPhone. We don't have accounts, we don't run servers that collect your data, and we don't track you, show ads, or sell anything about you.

What the app stores, and where

  • Repair orders, hours and settings are stored inside the app on your iPhone.
  • Photos and files you add to a repair order are stored inside the app on your iPhone. Files you choose to save go to the RO Log folder in the Files app, or wherever you pick in the share sheet.
  • Backups are files the app writes to its folder in the Files app. They're included in your iPhone's own iCloud or computer backup if you have that turned on.
  • RO Log is meant for repair order numbers, tags, VINs and vehicle details. It doesn't ask for customer names or addresses.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you tap a scan or photo button: to read an RO page, a repair sheet or a VIN, or to add a photo to a repair order. Text is read on your iPhone by Apple's built-in text recognition. The photo isn't sent anywhere.

Optional features that use the internet

  • Update check: about once a day the app asks Apple's App Store whether a newer version of RO Log is out. Nothing about you or your repair orders is sent.
  • VIN lookup: when you tap Look up vehicle, the VIN is sent to the U.S. government's free NHTSA vehicle database (vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov) to get the year, make and model.
  • Your own reader or cloud backup: if you choose to connect your own reader service in Settings, scan photos and an encrypted backup go to that service. This is off unless you set it up.

Sharing

Nothing leaves your iPhone unless you share it: a PDF, a backup file, a CSV export or a photo, using the iPhone share sheet.

Deleting your data

Delete any repair order in the app, or delete the app to remove everything stored inside it. Files you saved to the Files app stay there until you delete them.

Children

RO Log is a work tool for automotive technicians and isn't directed at children.
